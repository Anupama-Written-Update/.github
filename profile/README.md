### Prem's Father's Emotional Breakdown

In a new scene, Prem's father and mother are seen together. Prem's father, while holding a book, accidentally drops it. ### Prem's Mother Offers Support

Seeing her husband's distress, Prem's mother consoles him, saying, *"Everything will be fine, just give it some time."* She reassures him that things will work out eventually.

### Khyati's Promise to Bring Prem Back

Prem's father then turns to Khyati and pleads, *"Please bring my son back; otherwise, I will go into depression."* Hearing this, Khyati takes the responsibility upon herself and says, *"I will call him, Prem."* Her words bring a glimmer of hope to the family.
